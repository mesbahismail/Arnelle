# Demo assets

- Photography: Unsplash image URLs are recorded in `native/src/data.ts`. Local preview copies are in `public/media/`. Images are demo content, distributed under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), separately from the code's MIT license. Do not imply endorsement by the people depicted. Replace with your own media for a product launch.
- Illustrated SVG avatars: original procedural illustrations in `avatarSvg`, MIT licensed with this template.
- Native sample video: Big Buck Bunny, Blender Foundation, [CC BY 3.0](https://peach.blender.org/about/), hosted at the Expo sample-media CDN. This is a playback demonstration, not a live stream.
- Live-room nature demo: [flower-and-bee video](https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-videos/flower.mp4), from MDN’s CC0 sample media. Looped prerecorded footage, not an actual live broadcast.
- Audio: an original synthesized 30-second demo melody generated locally in `native/src/media.ts`. Track titles and artists are fictional UI data; they all preview this melody. Audio generation requires the native filesystem; the browser preview does not synthesize music.
- Icons: Ionicons (MIT) through Expo. The original Arnelle bird vector is in `native/src/brand.tsx`, MIT licensed.
- Fonts: platform system fonts.

Remote images and video need internet connectivity. The iPhone browser preview renders the same remote media as the native app. No original artwork from the supplied reference screenshots is included.

- Welcome forest photograph: [Unsplash image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1448375240586-882707db888b), under the Unsplash License.
